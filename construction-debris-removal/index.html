Construction Debris Removal FAQ

Straightforward answers about the process, pricing and service area.

How does construction debris removal pricing work?

V.I.P. provides a free estimate and reviews the price before approved work begins. Pricing can depend on volume, labor, access and the material involved.

Do I need to move the items outside first?

For approved full-service jobs, the V.I.P. crew handles the lifting and loading. Share any access details when requesting the estimate.

What areas do you serve?

V.I.P. serves Essex County and surrounding New Jersey communities, with priority coverage around Millburn, Short Hills, Livingston, Montclair, Glen Ridge, the Caldwells, South Orange, Maplewood and West Orange.

Can I request a larger cleanout?

Yes. The current V.I.P. service mix includes jobs ranging from bulky-item removal to larger property, estate and cleanout projects.
